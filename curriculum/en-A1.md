## Gramàtica

### a1.subject_pronouns — Subject pronouns [core]
Can do: Say who does something without repeating names.
Depth: light
Weight: 2
Forms: I, you, he (a man), she (a woman), it (a thing or an animal), we, you, they.
Tags: #pronouns, #grammar, i, you, he, she, it, we, they
Signals: he, she, it, we, they, pronoun, pronouns, mother, father, friend, friends, boy, girl, man, woman, people, name, names
Check:
- Complete: Anna is my sister. ___ is a nurse. → She
- Complete: Tom and I are friends. ___ play football together. → We
- Correct: The book is on the table. He is new. → The book is on the table. It is new.
